Moab Watersheds

A growing record of
our local creeks.

Pack Creek is the first stream station in this watershed monitoring network. Additional creek stations will join as they are installed.

Observations arrive through a low-power radio mesh. Streamflow is a rating-curve estimate; values outside the measured rating range are labeled as extrapolated. Stage is relative to the station’s local calibration.
