Methods

Stage is measured by ultrasonic or HOBO pressure sensors. Pack Creek discharge is calculated from its station rating curve. Cottonwood Creek has no discharge rating curve yet. Estimates outside the measured rating range are identified as extrapolated.

The HOBO MX2001 measures water temperature and pressure-based stage. Cottonwood Relay reports air temperature when temperature telemetry is available. Sensor records are independent. Times are shown in Mountain Time; CSV files also include UTC.

Chart lines are interrupted when observations are more than three hours apart.
