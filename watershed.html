Methods

Stage is measured by ultrasonic or HOBO pressure sensors. Pack Creek discharge is calculated from its station rating curve. Cottonwood Creek has no discharge rating curve yet. Estimates outside the measured rating range are identified as extrapolated.

The HOBO MX2001 measures water temperature and pressure-based stage. Cottonwood Relay reports air temperature when temperature telemetry is available. Sensor records are independent. Times are shown in Mountain Time; CSV files also include UTC.

Solid chart segments connect actual readings. Dashed amber links show linear connections across missing intervals and are not observed values. Gaps exceeding 3 hours are highlighted, increasing to 12 hours in the sampled 90-day view and 48 hours in the sampled 1-year view. Long-range graphs use one measured observation per sensor type per 6 hours or day, respectively; their CSV downloads contain displayed samples only.

Short-term arrows compare discharge over a recent 3-hour window and water or air temperature over a 6-hour window, only when sufficiently fresh observations exist. These arrows show recent change, not a forecast or a long-term statistical trend.

Methods

Stations are matched by MLID first, then by a cleaned station name when the workbook has no valid MLID. The original source name, notes and workbook row are retained with each record.

Values and reported precision are preserved from the Southeast Utah workbook. Field units are stated where defined by the workbook columns. The workbook does not provide units for laboratory chemistry and radiological columns, so the site does not invent them.
