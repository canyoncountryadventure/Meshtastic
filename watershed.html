Methods

Stage is measured by the SEN0313 ultrasonic sensor. Discharge is calculated from the station rating curve. Estimates outside the measured rating range are identified as extrapolated.

The HOBO MX2001 measures water temperature and pressure-based reference stage. Sensor records are independent. Times are shown in Mountain Time; CSV files also include UTC.

Chart lines are interrupted when observations are more than three hours apart.
